Best price Above fair (value) M priced from a milestone (15+ = Over 14.5)

Fair is the no-vig price: each book's over/under pair at a line is turned into a probability with its margin removed, then averaged across books.

Value bets compare a book's price with the fair price of the other books (at least books, edge from ). Arbitrage lines pay out on both sides when you take the best over and the best under at different books.

Prices move. Re-run scrape_odds.py, compare_odds.py and build_odds_page.py before betting, and check each book's rules (overtime, player not playing) for milestone lines.
