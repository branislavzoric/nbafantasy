Arbitrage

Best over and best under at different books add up to a guaranteed return. Stakes are split so both legs pay the same.

M Milestone price: the book only offers “N+” (for example 3+ assists), priced here as over N−0.5. Settlement rules can differ from a normal over/under, so check them before betting.

Edge = odds ÷ fair odds − 1. Fair odds come from the other books on the same line with their margin removed, so a book never sets its own fair price.
